# Muse icon concept v1

Generated using the built-in image_gen tool. Concept asset; application icon references have not been changed.

## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: production-quality square desktop app icon for Muse, a local-first visual inspiration library used by photographers and designers to collect, organize and rediscover images.
Create ONE finished icon, not a presentation board or multiple options.
Design direction: a distinctive geometric capital M formed from two overlapping folded photographic cards, with a tiny diamond-shaped negative-space aperture at the central fold. The M should feel like an elegant sculptural ribbon and a collection of images at once. Use broad confident shapes, excellent optical balance, clear recognizability at 32px. Bespoke restrained premium creative-tool identity.
Composition: front-on square icon, a large rounded-square charcoal tile (#15171a) occupying 88% of canvas, centered with even transparent margins. Within it a single centered abstract M symbol occupying about 62% of tile width. Two gently overlapping panels with cool violet (#8b70f6), periwinkle and electric blue (#4c74ff). Fold surfaces distinguished with a restrained smooth gradient; subtle dimensional layering, almost flat vector craftsmanship. Fine understated edge illumination, soft restrained inner lighting. No busy texture.
Mood: calm, refined, creative, precise, confident; fits a dark charcoal desktop gallery interface.
Constraints: truly transparent background outside the rounded-square tile. Icon only. NO wordmark, NO text, NO letters other than the abstract M silhouette, NO watermark, NO extra standalone sparkles, NO camera glyph, NO mountains, NO lens, NO neon halo, NO glassmorphism, NO mockup, NO surrounding objects. The design should remain bold and legible as a small Windows taskbar icon. Render with crisp immaculate edges at high resolution.
